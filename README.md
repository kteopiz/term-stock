# Stock Tracker (working title)

## Overview

This application will _eventually_ be a fast and lightweight TUI that can mimic your portfolio and show you historically or in real time how individual stocks in your portfolio are performing against one another.

I have many motivations for this project, all of which are pretty selfish, as you'll soon see XD.

First and foremost, this project is for personal education I have been a front end developer my entire internship career and I am ready for some change (I also am just not fond of front end development anymore). I hope to extend my skills and future job opportunities into real backend development and llearn a language that's commonly used for backend development. I selected Go because of a developer friend who LOVES Go and personally to avoid Java.

This project is also for personal use and entertainment. I am an active investor and I love just checking my portfolio for the fun of it at any given moment of the day. However, I find that apps like Wealthsimple or Questrade are really good at showing you how your ENTIRE portfolio is doing, but not which individual stocks are actually driving your portfolio's performance that day. The result? A project designed for me to enjoy working on it, and be useful for me in the future.

Rather than designing everything up front, I'm developing the project incrementally—adding features, refactoring when appropriate, and focusing on writing clean, idiomatic Go. The goal is iteration and improvement as I get better at Go/backend and learn best practices.

---

## Goals

The primary goal of this project is to deepen my understanding of Go while building something practical.

Along the way I hope to:

- Learn idiomatic Go and its standard library
- Become more comfortable building backend applications
- Practice writing clean, maintainable code
- Explore common backend concepts such as APIs, concurrency, caching, persistence, and application architecture
- Document important engineering decisions and lessons learned throughout development
- Have a way for me to look at my stocks anytime, anywhere, wicked fast.
